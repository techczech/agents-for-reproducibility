# Retrospective Notes

What helped:
- Weekly board meetings were short and decision-focused.
- Keeping payments out of pilot one protected the delivery date.
- Service representatives gave practical feedback on labels and testing slots.
- The supplier's weekly demos made progress visible.

What hurt:
- Staff testing was planned too late and clashed with service workload.
- The API field list was treated as a small admin task but became a dashboard blocker.
- Training material started after UAT planning, so it felt rushed.
- Retention wording was vague in early drafts.

Quotes:
- "We should have booked testers before the prototype was ready."
- "The dashboard was not hard; agreeing what it needed to show was hard."
- "The form worked better once we stopped using internal labels."
