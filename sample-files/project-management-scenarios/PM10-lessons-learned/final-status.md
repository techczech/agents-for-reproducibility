# Final Status

The pilot launched on 2026-06-19, two days later than the original target. The delay was accepted by the sponsor to allow accessibility fixes and staff onboarding to complete. Payments remained out of scope. The dashboard launched with the minimum agreed fields, and the older-request CSV export was treated as a follow-up enhancement.

Feedback after two weeks showed fewer duplicate booking emails and better visibility of open requests. Staff still wanted clearer guidance on exceptions and handoffs.
