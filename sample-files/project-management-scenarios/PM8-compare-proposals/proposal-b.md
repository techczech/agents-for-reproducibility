# Proposal B: CivicFlow Systems

Price: GBP 42,000 estimated, time and materials, cap to be agreed.

Delivery approach: discovery week followed by configuration. Says pilot date is possible but "subject to scope confirmation". Suggests adding online payments during pilot to avoid rework.

Functional fit: strong workflow engine, configurable routing, audit trail, dashboard, and automated reminders. Includes payment integration in proposed scope.

Accessibility: automated accessibility scan included. Manual review available as an extra-cost option.

Reporting/data: stronger standard dashboards than Proposal A. Retention settings are available, but proposal does not explain how incomplete requests are handled.

Support: standard support desk for three months. Handover documentation is "lightweight".

Concern: proposal may be over-scoped for the pilot and commercial cap is not yet defined.
