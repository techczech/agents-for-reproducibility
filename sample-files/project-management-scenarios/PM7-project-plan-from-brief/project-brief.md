# Project Brief

The Customer Service Booking Portal should reduce booking requests handled through shared inboxes. The pilot will cover two service areas and should prove whether a structured form, routing workflow, staff dashboard, and basic reporting can reduce duplicate requests and make status visible.

Expected pilot date: 2026-06-17.

High-level deliverables:
- public-facing booking request form,
- internal routing rules,
- staff dashboard,
- confirmation emails,
- reporting extract,
- staff guidance,
- pilot evaluation note.

The sponsor wants a plan that can be understood quickly and used to keep the supplier, service owner, and internal team aligned.
