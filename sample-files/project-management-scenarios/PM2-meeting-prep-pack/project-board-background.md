# Project Board Background

The project board meets weekly during the pilot build. Its job is to unblock delivery decisions, not review every workstream. The standing attendees are the sponsor, service owner, data protection adviser, supplier delivery lead, business analyst, and project manager.

The go-live date for the pilot is 2026-06-17. The board has already agreed that the pilot must include accessibility fixes, clear service routing, staff guidance, and basic reporting. It has not agreed whether payments or advanced analytics are in scope.
