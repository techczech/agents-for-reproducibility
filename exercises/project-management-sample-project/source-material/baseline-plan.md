# Baseline Plan

Pilot scope:
- booking request form,
- routing workflow,
- staff dashboard,
- confirmation emails,
- reporting extract,
- staff guidance and training,
- evaluation note after four weeks.

Out of scope:
- payments,
- advanced analytics,
- integrations with finance systems,
- public account creation.

Target pilot date: 2026-06-17.

Budget: GBP 50,000 supplier budget plus internal team time.
