# Produce Sponsor Decision Pack

## Summary

Use the source material to produce a sponsor-ready change-impact brief.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Brief names the source files used.
- Impacts cover scope, schedule, cost, quality, data protection, training, and stakeholder communications.
- Options include pros and cons.
- Recommendation distinguishes evidence from judgement.
- Sponsor wording is concise and ready to adapt.
