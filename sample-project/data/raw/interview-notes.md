# Fictional Interview Notes

These excerpts are fictional and created for a workshop exercise. They are not drawn from real participants.

## Excerpt I01

Role: doctoral researcher in social sciences

"I use AI mostly when I am stuck at the beginning. It helps me turn a messy idea into a few possible structures. I would not trust it to decide what my data means, but it can help me see what I need to clarify."

## Excerpt I02

Role: research support officer

"The biggest problem is not whether the tool can produce a summary. The problem is knowing what was uploaded, what was changed, and whether anyone can reconstruct the steps later."

## Excerpt I03

Role: lab-based researcher

"I can see the value for routine scripts and file organisation. But we have data that cannot leave approved systems. I need a workflow where the tool only sees safe examples or already approved files."

## Excerpt I04

Role: principal investigator

"People talk about AI as if it replaces judgement. In practice the useful question is more boring: did it save the assistant three hours of formatting, and can we still tell what was done?"

## Excerpt I05

Role: master's student

"I used a chatbot to explain a paper, but then I could not remember exactly what I asked or which parts came from the source. It was useful at the time but hard to cite or revisit."
